## The brief

Monsoon Sessions is a fictional two-night outdoor music series held in the rainy season, on purpose. The organizers were managing sign-ups through a form and a spreadsheet, and answering the same three questions on WhatsApp all day: who is playing, when, and how do I get in.

## What we designed

- **A loud, joyful identity** in Night Bloom: midnight indigo, acid lime, violet rain
- **A lineup and schedule** you can scan in five seconds
- **Registration in three short steps**, with the total always visible
- **A site built for launch-day traffic**, served from the edge so it stays fast when the lineup drops

## The lineup and registration

Switch between nights, then register. Each step checks your input before letting you continue, and you can go back without losing anything.

## Why it works

Every common question is answered on the page, so the organizers' phones go quiet. Registration takes under a minute, and the flow is built so a real payment and ticketing service can be connected later without a redesign.
