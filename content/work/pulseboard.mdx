## The brief

Pulseboard is a dashboard for a fictional online business that had outgrown its spreadsheets: orders in one file, revenue in another, and a weekly scramble to work out whether things were actually going well.

The goal: one screen that answers **"how are we doing?"** in five seconds, and lets the team find any order in two.

## What we designed

- **Three numbers that matter**, each compared with the previous period, so every figure has context
- **A revenue chart** you can hover, or step through with the arrow keys, with the same data available as a table
- **An orders table** that filters by status and searches by customer instantly, with no page reloads
- **A layout that works on a phone**, because owners check their numbers away from a desk

## Try the dashboard

## Why it works

A dashboard is only useful if people trust it and can read it at a glance. Every number has a comparison, every chart has a table behind it, and nothing depends on colour alone. The same front-end connects to a real database or API when it goes live.
