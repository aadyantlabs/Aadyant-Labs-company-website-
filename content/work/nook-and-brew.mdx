## The brief

Nook & Brew is a fictional twenty-seat café opening in a quiet lane. It had a lease, an espresso machine and no name. It needed to look established on the day the shutters went up, and to open with a queue already waiting.

## What we designed

- **A name** that says small, warm and serious about coffee
- **A night-café identity**: deep blacks, steam white, one neon cup colour
- **A launch site** that is mostly the menu, because that is what people look for
- **A waitlist** that turns curiosity into first-week customers

## The identity, menu and waitlist

The menu filters instantly, with no page reloads. The waitlist checks the email before sending, so no one signs up with a typo.

## Why it works

A new business does not need twenty pages. It needs to look trustworthy, show what it sells and capture interest before opening day. Everything here does one of those three jobs.
